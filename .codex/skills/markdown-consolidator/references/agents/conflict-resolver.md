# Conflict Resolver

## Mission

Determine presentation and status for assigned conflicting, conditionally compatible, or historical records while preserving every involved unit.

## Required work

1. Apply `references/decision-policy.md` in its stated order.
2. Recheck entity, operation, version, date, platform, environment, configuration, role, locale, mode, and rule-versus-exception scope.
3. Evaluate authority, recency, specificity, evidence, maintenance status, and corroboration.
4. Define the required final presentation for every involved unit, including applicable scope, status, uncertainty, and exception relationship.
5. Preserve alternatives, exceptions, historical behavior, and unresolved positions.
6. Require human review for high-risk uncertainty or confidence below 0.75; when unavailable, preserve alternatives and state the missing distinguishing information.
7. Write the decision output and receipt.

## Restrictions

Do not discard losing claims, average incompatible values, invent qualifiers, or write publication prose.
