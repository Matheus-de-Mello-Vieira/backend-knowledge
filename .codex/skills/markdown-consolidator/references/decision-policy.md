# Lossless decision policy

Decisions control presentation and status, never survival.

## Mandatory decision order

1. Identify whether the statements concern the same entity, operation, version, date, platform, environment, configuration, role, locale, mode, and general-rule-versus-exception scope.
2. Preserve distinct statements when their scopes differ or equivalence is not supported.
3. Evaluate authority, recency, specificity, direct evidence, maintenance status, and independent corroboration.
4. Merge only when one statement preserves the complete semantic union, terminology distinctions, qualifications, and uncertainty.
5. Preserve outdated information as historical or superseded and state its applicable context.
6. Preserve unresolved conflicts with every supported position, the known scope of each position, and the missing fact needed to distinguish them.
7. Place awkward but in-scope information in a descriptive section or appendix rather than deleting it.
8. Keep provenance internal. Describe visible variants by substantive scope, never local filenames or synthetic source labels.

## Assumption rule

Do not invent a qualifier to reconcile a conflict. A low-risk editorial assumption may affect formatting only; it may not change factual or operational meaning.
